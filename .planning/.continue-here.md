unemployment reaches 1040 line 8 via Schedule 1 line 7, which was a declared zero whose only
scope kind was a coarse catch-all. The refusal working on a first real document was the most
valuable single event of the session.

  line 7 now real.

  gained the 1099-G term. `inputsOf` (test helper) keeps ten curried params and defaults the

- `kindVocabulary` 50 → 51, `modeledKinds` 20 → 21, `finance_schema` 9 → 10,
  `fjs/media/dialects` 13 → 14 — all in one commit, per the Phase 8/11/12 same-commit precedent.
