<objective>
Revise the Phase 6 guest ABI (`fjs/guest/module.f.js`) per 07-CONTEXT.md Decision 1: widen
`guestCtx` with the composition combinators (`step`, `pure`) and the money helpers
(`centsFromString`, `centsToString`) that EXEC-07's own requirement text names but that Phase
6 never delivered. `CasOp` and `casOpNames` — the frozen four read-only commands — do not
change; only `ctx` grows non-operation members. This is necessary infrastructure for Success
Criterion 1: summing `box1InterestIncome` across every stored 1099-INT requires looping over an
unknown number of documents, which is impossible with zero imports and no sequencing primitive.

Purpose: without this, no Week-1-shaped program can be authored at all — Phase 6 shipped a
`guestCtx` with four unary command constructors and nothing to compose more than one of them.
Output: `guestCtx` carries `casRead, evoList, evoHead, evoRevision, step, pure,
centsFromString, centsToString`; the two Phase 6 proofs that assumed an exact four-key ctx are
revised to distinguish *commands* (still exactly four, still a live equality assertion) from
*combinators* (additive, never operations).
</objective>
