## Phase Boundary

A brokerage sale flows all the way to line 16 — replacing the refusal that stands there
today. `vnd.fjs.1099b` and `vnd.fjs.1099div` (Phase 12) supply the input; this phase
builds Form 8949, Schedule D, and the Schedule D Tax Worksheet, and wires the result into
Form 1040 lines 3a, 3b and 7a.

**In scope:** Form 8949 categorization and totals; Schedule D including lines 15, 16, 18
17, 18, 19, 20, 21 and 22; the Schedule D Tax Worksheet; the scope reclassification of
**six** kinds; the
Form 1040 wiring of lines 3a/3b/7a; the demo update that `tsc` forces.

**Out of scope:** Form 4952 / investment interest election (stays refusing, deliberately —
see Decision 1.2); wash-sale and accrued-market-discount *adjustments* (refused at the
dialect level, see 1.4); Schedule 1/2/3, Schedule A, Schedule 8812 (Phase 13).

### A verified defect in this phase's own success criteria — read before planning

**ROADMAP criterion 2a is incomplete, and criterion 2 is unsatisfiable without widening
it.** Established by reading `fjs/tax/line16/module.f.js`, not inferred:

The `scheduleDTaxWorksheet` method is reachable from exactly two branches:

| Branch | Condition | Today |
|---|---|---|
| 2a | `filingScheduleD && D15>0 && D16>0 && (D18>0 \|\| D19>0)` | refuses — `collectibles28RateGain` / `unrecaptured1250Gain` |
| 2b | `filingForm4952 && form4952Line4gCents > 0n` | refuses — `investmentInterestForm4952` |

Branches 2c, 2d and 2e — qualified dividends, capital gain distributions, and Schedule D
lines 15/16 both gains — **all route to the QDCGT**, which already computes.

Criterion 2a names only `qualifiedDividends` and `ordinaryDividends`. Both reach the
dispatcher at branch 2c, which returns `qdcgtOutcome()`. **Modeling only those two can
never make the `scheduleDTaxWorksheet` branch produce a figure**, so criterion 2 would
remain false with criterion 2a fully satisfied.

Criterion 1 already implies the wider set and does not need amending: 1099-DIV **box 2b**
is unrecaptured §1250 gain, **box 2c** is §1202 gain, **box 2d** is collectibles 28%-rate
gain. The criteria agree with each other; it is 2a's *enumeration* that is partial.

This is the third instance in this project of planning text contradicting shipped code.
It was found by reading the dispatcher rather than by trusting the roadmap.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Scope — what becomes modeled

- **1.1 — SIX kinds move from unmodeled to modeled** (amended from seven), in one atomic
  change: `qualifiedDividends` (3a), `ordinaryDividends` (3b), `capitalGainDistributions`
  (7a), `capitalGainsOrLosses` (7a), `unrecaptured1250Gain` (Sch D 19),
  `collectibles28RateGain` (Sch D 18).
  `unrecaptured1250Gain` **or** `collectibles28RateGain` is what makes the
  `scheduleDTaxWorksheet` branch reachable — necessary and sufficient. Counts:
  `modeledKinds` 6 → **12**, `unmodeledKindRefusals` 44 → **38**, `kindVocabulary` stays
  **50**. 12 + 38 = 50.
- **1.2 — TWO kinds stay UNMODELED and keep refusing**, for different reasons:
  - `investmentInterestForm4952` — criterion 1 does not name Form 4952, and keeping a live
    refusal *on the Schedule D Tax Worksheet branch itself* is the proof that TAX-16 still
    works after that branch starts computing. A branch that can only succeed is no longer
    evidence of a guard.
  - `section1202Gain` — the exclusion percentage is not in any 1099-DIV box, so the engine
    **cannot** compute the right number. Passing box 2c through without the exclusion would
    overstate tax for anyone entitled to it. Refusing is the honest option and the smaller
    one. The §1202 exclusion mechanism is explicitly out of scope for this phase.
- **1.5 — Schedule D lines 17, 20, 21 and 22 are IN SCOPE** (added after research). Line 21
  is the capital loss limitation: the lesser of the net loss and **$3,000** ($1,500 if
  married filing separately). Form 1040 line 7a must be correct for all three outcomes —
  net gain, zero, and capped loss. A loss year is ordinary, not exotic. Carryover *to a
  future year* is not modeled here (it is a next-year input, not a TY2025 output).
- **1.6 — `filingScheduleD` derives from the DECLARED KIND** `capitalGainsOrLosses`, not
  from the presence of a `vnd.fjs.1099b` document. Kind declaration is the load-bearing
  signal throughout this codebase, and the scope guard already prevents a declared kind
  from reaching computation without the documents to satisfy it. A taxpayer with only
  capital gain distributions and no 1099-B does not file Schedule D — that is dispatcher
  branch 2d, which already works and must keep working.
- **1.3 — Form 1040 line 7a is wired in the SAME change as 3a and 3b.** `line7a` is
  `declaredZero` today (`fjs/form1040/core/module.f.js`), and capital gains are TAX-11's
  entire subject. It carries the identical reclassify-without-wiring hazard.
- **1.4 — 1099-B box 1f (accrued market discount) and box 1g (wash sale loss disallowed)
  are refused at the DIALECT level; the Form 8949 adjustment is deferred.** The 50-kind
  vocabulary is frozen and has no kind for either, so a *scope* refusal is not
  expressible. Ignoring them would compute a silently wrong gain, which is the one
  outcome this engine exists to prevent. Refusing is honest and small; the adjustment is
  a later phase's work.

### Module layout and worksheet decomposition

- **2.1 — New modules:** `fjs/form8949/module.f.js` and `fjs/schedule/d/module.f.js`,
  following the `fjs/schedule/b` precedent Phase 12 set.
- **2.2 — The Schedule D Tax Worksheet lives at `fjs/tax/line16/sdtw/module.f.js`**,
  mirroring `fjs/tax/line16/qdcgt/`. It is a line-16 method, and the sibling layout makes
  the two directly comparable — which matters, because the 2a-before-2c ordering between
  them is the subtlest rule in the dispatcher.
- **2.3 — TAX-15 shape, strictly:** one named pure function per worksheet, carrying the
  printed form's line numbers, in IRS order. **No variable named `magi`.**
- **2.4 — The Form 8949 category (A–F) is DERIVED**, from `box12BasisReportedToIrs` ×
  `box2ShortTermGainOrLoss`/`box2LongTermGainOrLoss` — and then **asserted to agree with
  the payer-printed `applicableCheckboxOnForm8949` when that field is present.** The
  payer's letter is a second source a proof can watch drift, never the input itself.
  Trusting it directly would make the engine's own categorization unverifiable.
- **2.5 — The 28% Rate Gain Worksheet and the Unrecaptured §1250 Gain Worksheet model only
  the lines this project's dialects can populate** — line 4 and line 11 respectively — with
  every other line hardcoded to its zero-equivalent and **the boundary stated in the
  module's own docstring**. This mirrors `fjs/schedule/b`'s Form 8815 boundary precedent.
  A line that cannot be populated is not silently omitted; it is written down as a
  documented zero, so a later phase widening the dialect knows exactly what to revisit.
- **2.6 — An absent 1099-B box 1e (cost basis) produces a graceful, tagged refusal**, shaped
  like the existing `Form1040Error` / `Line16Outcome` error arm — a `{ kind: 'error', ... }`
  result that Form 8949 / Schedule D return and `fjs/form1040/core` threads through exactly
  as it already threads `line16Outcome`'s error arm. **Not** an `assert` crash, and **not**
  a new entry in the frozen 50-kind vocabulary: this is a document-data-sufficiency problem,
  not a declared-kind scope problem. **This is also the production surface criterion 3a
  needs** — the mutation that conflates absent basis with zero basis must redden a real
  proof of this refusal.

### Proofs, gates, and downstream effects

- **3.1 — DOC-07 is re-proven against REAL gain computation (criterion 3a).** A mutation
  in *production* code that conflates absent basis with zero basis must turn a *real*
  proof red, watched failing. Phase 12's M3 reddened a fixture assertion sitting in front
  of the comparison it appeared to guard; that is not the same thing and must not repeat.
- **3.2 — RESOLVED: the Schedule D Tax Worksheet is cent-exact.** `i1040sd.pdf` p.9 states
  whole-dollar rounding is the taxpayer's *optional election* ("You can round off cents to
  whole dollars on your Schedule D. If you do round to whole dollars, you must round all
  amounts."), identical to Form 1040 p.23 — not a worksheet-specific mandate, and there is
  no separate rounding instruction for the SDTW itself. Build cent-exact exactly like the
  QDCGT and let the existing report-level `applyWholeDollarElection` apply uniformly.
  **Do not add a second, worksheet-local rounding rule.** Verified against the printed
  form, not assumed; the fallback this decision originally carried never fires.
- **3.3 — The demo is updated inside this phase, because the build forces it.**
  `demo/steps/04-refusal.js` declares `const offered: readonly UnmodeledKind[]` listing
  `'qualifiedDividends'` and `'capitalGainsOrLosses'` — both leave that union here, so
  **`tsc` fails until it is fixed**. Replace them with kinds drawn from the 37 that still
  refuse, and re-read the narration against what the engine now does. The demo's general
  claim ("declare something unmodeled and the whole return refuses") remains true.
- **3.4 — Worked expected values are hand-transcribed from the printed Schedule D Tax
  Worksheet and independently reproduced** by research, by the planner and by the
  executor before being written down — the Phase 10 QDCGT precedent. Never generated from
  the engine, which would be circular.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- **The 2a-before-2c ordering is the subtlest rule in the dispatcher and must survive
  this phase.** `fjs/tax/line16`'s docstring records why: with Schedule D lines 18 and 19
  both zero the two worksheets are algebraically identical, so no ordinary test case can
  distinguish the orderings — only the selected `method` tag can. A taxpayer with both
  qualified dividends and §1250 gain routed to the QDCGT gets the preferential slice taxed
  at 0/15/20% instead of up to 25%/28%: wrong, wrong in the taxpayer's favour, and silent.
  Now that both branches compute, the existing method-tag proofs are the only thing still
  holding this down. They must be strengthened, not merely preserved.

- **A recorded form-vs-instructions discrepancy comes due in this phase.** Schedule D
  line 20 asks "Are lines 18 and 19 both zero or blank AND you are not filing Form 4952?",
  which would route a Form 4952 filer with line 4g = 0 into the Schedule D Tax Worksheet.
  The line-16 instructions and the worksheet's own header both state the stricter
  `4952 filed AND 4g > 0`. The stricter form is implemented, and
  `fjs/tax/line16/module.f.js` says in comments that the discrepancy "matters for Phase 12,
  when that worksheet actually computes." That is now.

- **The 13/37/50 invariant is worth asserting**, not just the two counts independently.

## Deferred Ideas

- **Form 4952 / the investment interest expense election** — deliberately left refusing
  (Decision 1.2), and it is the live evidence that the guard still works.
- **The §1202 exclusion (1099-DIV box 2c)** — left refusing (Decision 1.2). Reopening it
  needs the exclusion percentage, which depends on when the *fund* acquired the QSB stock
  and is carried by no 1099-DIV box. Modeling it therefore requires a new document source,
  not merely new arithmetic.
- **Capital loss carryover TO a future year.** Line 21's cap is in scope; recording the
  unused remainder for next year's return is a multi-year concern this engine has no place
  to put yet.
- **Wash-sale (box 1g) and accrued-market-discount (box 1f) adjustments** — refused at the
  dialect level here (Decision 1.4); the Form 8949 adjustment-code machinery is a later
  phase. Candidate for Phase 15 (Realism Polish).
- **Section 897 boxes (1099-DIV 2e/2f)** — stored by the dialect, not read by this chain.
- **Form 8949 rows for property with no 1099-B** (categories C and F) — the dialect only
  models broker-reported sales.
- **Re-pinning the browser demo to a later release.** The demo's *content* is fixed here
  because `tsc` requires it; choosing a new pinned SHA and re-running the anchor audit is
  a separate, deliberate act (`demo/todo/showcase-demo.md`).
