<objective>
Form 1040 lines 1a through 15 (TAX-05, first half): income, adjusted gross income, the standard
deduction, and taxable income — every line a `ReportLine` citing the documents it derived from
(ROADMAP criterion 1), and line 2b carrying criterion 5's ten-document rounding demonstration.

The property that makes this honest rather than decorative: **a legitimately zero line still cites a
document.** The taxpayer declared no dividends, so line 3b is `0n` — with the return profile's own
`declaredKinds` box as its source, because that declaration is what makes the zero true. A line that
returns `0n` for an input the engine cannot model is a different thing entirely and never reaches
here: the scope guard (Plan 10-07) refuses the whole report first. The distinction is carried
entirely by the declared kind set, which is why the profile is load-bearing rather than convenient.

Purpose: line 15 is the input to every line-16 branch, and the source-union mechanism built here is
what makes line 16 transitively cite every document that fed it (PROV-02).
Output: `fjs/form1040/core/module.f.js` — lines 1a-15 and `unionSources`.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries
