<objective>
Make `qualifyingSurvivingSpouse` a first-class `IndividualFilingStatus` (10-CONTEXT.md Decision 6),
with its own stored TY2025 parameter rows, and absorb the fan-out that the `Record<FilingStatus, …>`
types force across `fjs/tax/boundary` and `fjs/server/finance_tax_params`.

Why this is a real change and not a rename: QSS's dollar amounts equal MFJ's everywhere, but its
**maximum age/blindness box count is 2, not 4** (10-CONTEXT.md D-06). Mapping QSS onto MFJ at a call
site would silently permit a 4-box standard deduction no QSS filer can claim. The box-count rule
itself lands in Plan 10-05; this plan lands the status it needs to attach to.

Purpose: every later plan in this phase (the standard deduction chart, the return-profile dialect's
`filingStatus` vocabulary, the Tax Table column mapping, QDCGT's line 6/13 breakpoints) resolves
filing status through this one type. It has to be right first.
Output: `IndividualFilingStatus` with five members; three new stored parameter rows; the threshold
inventory grown from 42 to 50 with every new threshold covered by the already-generated proof.
</objective>
