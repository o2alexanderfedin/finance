<objective>
Form 1040 line 12e (TAX-06): the standard deduction with age and blindness increments, the
Standard Deduction Worksheet for Dependents, and the exceptions that override both.

The trap this plan exists to close, stated once so it cannot be missed: **`single` and
`marriedFilingSeparately` share the same $15,750 base but take DIFFERENT increments** — $2,000 and
$1,600. Any implementation that derives the increment from the base, or that partitions statuses as
"unmarried = not married filing jointly", is wrong by $400 per checked box, up to $1,600 on a
four-box return. The governing rule is Rev. Proc. 2024-40 §2.15(3)'s wording, which
`fjs/tax/params`' field names compress lossily: the $2,000 amount applies to an individual who is
"unmarried **and not a surviving spouse**". So `$2,000` is single and head of household; `$1,600` is
married filing jointly, married filing separately, **and qualifying surviving spouse** — a QSS filer
is not married yet takes the `married` amount, which is exactly why the field names cannot be read
as the rule.

Research cross-checked the printed Standard Deduction Chart against `fjs/tax/params` and found **no
disagreement**, three independent ways (the Form 1040 face margin, the Dependents worksheet line 3,
and the chart by subtraction). 10-CONTEXT.md Decision 3 is therefore NOT triggered. If execution
finds a disagreement anyway, stop and report before changing anything — it would invalidate Phase 8's
proofs either way.

Purpose: line 12e feeds line 14, line 15, and therefore every line-16 branch.
Output: `fjs/tax/deduction/module.f.js` with all 19 chart combinations, both dependents-worksheet
arms, and the three exceptions, each expectation hand-typed.
</objective>
