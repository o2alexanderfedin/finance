<objective>
Close the half of the line-16 dispatch that lives below the worksheets: the **level-3 base lookup**
— Tax Table below $100,000, Tax Computation Worksheet at or above — as one function that returns
**which method it used**, not only a number.

Research's load-bearing claim, which this plan must VERIFY rather than assume: the TCW is not new
arithmetic. Its twenty printed rows are each `rate x taxableIncome - subtraction`, and every one of
the twenty subtraction constants is exactly `rate x bracketLowerBound - taxAt(bracketLowerBound)` —
i.e. the printed worksheet *is* `cumulativeBracketTaxCents`, which this module already ships and
already row-diffs for the Tax Table. So the branch's entire risk is a transcription diff plus a
boundary gate, and Task 1 exists to prove that claim on all twenty rows rather than on the five
spot checks research performed.

Two corrections research established that this plan carries: the Tax Computation Worksheet is on
**i1040gi printed page 80**, not p124 (p124 is the alphabetical index); and the Tax Table occupies
pp68-79.

Purpose: QDCGT lines 22 and 24 (Plan 10-06) each call back into this lookup **independently**, and
the dispatcher (Plan 10-08) uses it as its default arm. Both need the method tag, not just cents.
Output: `taxComputationWorksheet`, `taxTableColumnFor`, `baseTaxForAmount`, and the twenty-row
hand transcription, in `fjs/tax/table/module.f.js`.
</objective>
