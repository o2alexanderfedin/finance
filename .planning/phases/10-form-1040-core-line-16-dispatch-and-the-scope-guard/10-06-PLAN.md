<objective>
The Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet (i1040gi p38), all 25 lines — and with it
**ROADMAP criterion 2's regression proof, the single most valuable artifact this phase produces.**

10-CONTEXT.md Decision 1 pulls this forward from TAX-08 deliberately: criterion 2 demands a
regression proof for the signature $1-$12 line-16 error, and that proof cannot exist without QDCGT
actually computing, because the discrepancy arises precisely where QDCGT lines 22 and 24 call *back
into* the Tax Table, whose $25/$50 midpoint bands make the two paths disagree.

**Be precise about which bug is being pinned.** It is the omission of line 25's `min`, not naive Tax
Table use. An engine that simply looks line 1 up in the Tax Table gets *both* regression cases
right — that is exactly what line 24 computes and what `min` then selects. A regression proof aimed
at "naive Tax Table use" would pass green while the real defect shipped. The proof therefore has to
pin the *pair*, and it has to pin the case where `min` selects the other side too.

Purpose: this worksheet is the QDCGT arm of TAX-03's four-way dispatch (Plan 10-08) and the whole
of criterion 2.
Output: `fjs/tax/line16/qdcgt/module.f.js` — 25 named lines, four worked cases, all 25 values
hand-typed for the regression pair.
</objective>
