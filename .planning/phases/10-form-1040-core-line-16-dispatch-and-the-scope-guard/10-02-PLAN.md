<objective>
Model Form 1040's whole-dollar election (10-CONTEXT.md Decision 5) as a projection applied to a
whole report at once, and prove `round(sum)` diverges from `sum(round)` with the IRS's own printed
example value.

Why this plan exists at all: **ROADMAP criterion 5 is a tautology without it.** Money in computation
is `bigint` cents, so over cents `round(sum)` and `sum(round)` are both the identity and any proof
of their equality tests nothing. Rounding only bites at whole dollars, and whole-dollar rounding is
an *election* the IRS states in one sentence (i1040gi p23): "You **can** round off cents to whole
dollars... **If you do** round to whole dollars, you must round **all** amounts... If you have to add
two or more amounts to figure the amount to enter on a line, include cents when adding the amounts
and round off only the total."

Two properties come straight out of that sentence and both are structural here, not conventional:
all-or-nothing (so the projection takes the whole `ReportLine[]`, never one line), and round-the-total
(so the projection applies to a line whose value is already the exact cents sum).

Purpose: gives Plan 10-09 a shipped, independently proven election to apply over ten real 1099-INT
documents, instead of inventing one inside the biggest plan of the phase.
Output: `applyWholeDollarElection` in `fjs/report/line/module.f.js`, with the abstract $14-vs-$10
divergence proven here and the ten-document version proven in 10-09.
</objective>

    mechanism, which would be conspicuous.

    own `wholeDollarElection` box (Plan 10-04), never a host-side flag — so the election is a
