`fjs/tax/params`'s `Citation` type so every parameter this phase adds (statutory, not just

cap, CTC/ODC — CONTEXT.md Decision 6.2), and AGI includes 1040 line 6b (taxable Social
Security), so this worksheet must exist and be correct before anything downstream can be
trusted.

`fjs/tax/ssb/module.f.js` (new module, 18 lines, count-guard proof).
