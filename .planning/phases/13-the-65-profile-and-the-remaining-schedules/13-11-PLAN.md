## A finding from research that changes this wave's shape from CONTEXT.md's literal table

13-CONTEXT.md's Decision 6.1 table lists this slice's kinds among the phase's reclassifications.
13-RESEARCH.md's Open Questions 1 and 2 — read closely during this plan's preparation — establish
why that cannot be done honestly: `scheduleOneAdditionalIncome`, `scheduleOneAdjustments`,
`scheduleTwoTaxes`, `scheduleThreeNonrefundableCredits` and `scheduleThreeRefundableCredits` are
each ONE coarse kind covering MANY distinct Schedule 1/2/3 line items (Schedule 1 Part I alone
has 26 "other income" sub-lines), and this engine has no per-line dialect for ANY of them — no
1099-G, no Schedule C, no Form 6251, no Form 8960, no Schedule R. A taxpayer who genuinely has
one of these items and declares the coarse kind cannot have their real dollar figure represented
at all; reclassifying the kind to "modeled" while the module can only ever return `$0` would be
exactly TAX-16's failure mode — a confident zero replacing an honest refusal. Research's own
Open Question 2 recommendation states it plainly: "No action needed for Phase 13's target
profile" — these five kinds STAY REFUSED. This plan and Plan 13-12 build and wire the modules
(satisfying criterion 4's "carry every line" for the profile that CAN compute); Plan 13-13
corrects these five kinds' remedy strings to stop promising "Phase 13" delivers what it
structurally cannot.

Output: `fjs/schedule/1/module.f.js`, `fjs/schedule/2/module.f.js`, `fjs/schedule/3/module.f.js`
(three new modules).
