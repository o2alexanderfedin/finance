<objective>
Close the coverage gaps a systematic mutation sweep found in `fjs/tax/*`, `fjs/exact` and
`fjs/types/*`. 30 deliberate defects were introduced one at a time; **11 survived undetected**, of
which the sweep honestly classified 2 as semantic no-ops, leaving **9 real gaps**.

The largest: **bracket data above roughly $100,000 has essentially no verification.**
`fjs/tax/table`'s transcribed rows stop at $99,999.99 and `finance_tax_params` spot-checks only
MFJ's first bracket. The 32%, 35% and 37% ceilings and rates — for single, HoH, MFS and estates &
trusts — can all be silently wrong. These are dollar figures a taxpayer's liability depends on.
</objective>

<the_primary_source_figures>
**These were read by the orchestrator directly from the rendered pages of Rev. Proc. 2024-40**
(`https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-24-40.pdf`, §2.01 Tables 1–5 on pages 5–7, and §2.03 on
pages 7–8). Type them into the proofs as literals.

This matters for independence: asserting against `08-RESEARCH.md` would only catch drift between
research and code. These come from the governing document itself, so they catch an error in the

**§2.01 ordinary rate schedules — bracket UPPER bounds ("not over"), by rate:**

| Rate | MFJ / surviving spouse | Head of household | Single | MFS | Estates & trusts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | 23,850 | 17,000 | 11,925 | 11,925 | 3,150 |
| 12% | 96,950 | 64,850 | 48,475 | 48,475 | *(none)* |
| 22% | 206,700 | 103,350 | 103,350 | 103,350 | *(none)* |
| 24% | 394,600 | 197,300 | 197,300 | 197,300 | 11,450 |
| 32% | 501,050 | 250,500 | 250,525 | 250,525 | *(none)* |
| 35% | 751,600 | 626,350 | 626,350 | 375,800 | 15,650 |
| 37% | *(no ceiling)* | *(no ceiling)* | *(no ceiling)* | *(no ceiling)* | *(no ceiling)* |

Note three things the printed tables show and that a careless transcription would get wrong:
- **Estates & trusts has only four rates** — 10, 24, 35, 37. There is no 12% or 22% bracket.
- **Single and MFS diverge only at the top**: identical through 32% (250,525), then MFS's 35%
  bracket ends at 375,800 while single's ends at 626,350.
- **HoH's 32% ceiling is 250,500**, twenty-five dollars below single/MFS's 250,525. Not a typo.

**§2.03 maximum capital gains rate amounts:**

| Filing status | Max zero-rate | Max 15% rate |
|---|---|---|
| MFJ / surviving spouse | 96,700 | 600,050 |
| MFS | 48,350 | 300,000 |
| Head of household | 64,750 | 566,700 |
| All other individuals (single) | 48,350 | 533,400 |
| Estates and trusts | 3,250 | 15,900 |

Every figure above matches what `fjs/tax/params/module.f.js` already stores. **The data is correct;
it is simply unproven.** Do not change any stored value — if a figure disagrees with this table,
stop and report it rather than "fixing" either side.
